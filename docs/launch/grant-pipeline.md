# Grant pipeline

*Checked 12 October 2026 on each funder's own page or document. "Verified" means the deadline and eligibility were
read on the primary source that day. "Partial" means something is still unconfirmed, and the notes say what. Always
re-check the funder's page before you apply; dates move.*

## The one thing that decides most of these: who is applying

Most funders need a legal entity to pay. AGENCY has none yet. Four routes, and which funders each unlocks:

| Route | Unlocks | Doesn't unlock |
|---|---|---|
| **UCSC, with a faculty or staff member as PI or advisor** (students can't be PI at UCSC) | CITRIS Tech for Social Good, VentureWell, NIDILRR, PVA Education (a UCSC employee as Project Director), PVA Research | Funders that want an independent nonprofit; and UC may have views on IP |
| **A fiscal sponsor** (weeks) | Fast Forward and Echoing Green (both say sponsored projects are fine); possibly the ALS Association and Neilsen if the sponsor applies and holds the IP | Reeve ("Fiscal Sponsors are NOT allowed"); funders that ask for your own IRS letter |
| **Your own nonprofit corporation** (days to incorporate in California) | The ALS Association, if it controls the IP (the RFA doesn't require 501(c)(3) status); NIDILRR (nonprofits with or without 501(c)(3) are eligible) | Funders that need an IRS determination letter, until you have one |
| **Your own 501(c)(3) with an IRS letter** (months; see `docs/NONPROFIT_PLAN.md`) | Everything here, including Reeve, AWS Imagine, Ability Central, Neilsen | |
