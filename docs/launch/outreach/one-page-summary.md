# AGENCY

**Free, open-source eye control for the Mac, for people who can only move their eyes.**

`[[TEAM: website]]` · `[[TEAM: email]]` · github.com/NeilGilani/game

## The problem

Most people with ALS lose the ability to meet their daily communication needs with speech (80 to 95%, Beukelman et
al. 2011). Eye gaze often lasts longest, but everyday eye typing runs at about 8 words a minute (Cai et al. 2023),
and most eye-gaze tools help people talk, not get things done.

## What AGENCY does

- **Eye control with the laptop's own camera.** No special hardware. Also works with head movement, a long blink
  or a switch.
- **Talk.** Phrase boards and an eye keyboard that speak aloud, with optional AI to finish sentences faster.
- **Get things done.** An AI helper plans everyday tasks and shows exactly what it will do. Nothing happens until
  the person approves, and Stop always works.
- **Call for help.** Three long blinks start an alert that keeps contacting chosen family members until one says
  they're coming. It is not a medical alarm or emergency service.

## Where it stands (October 2026)

| Works today | Not yet |
|---|---|
| Mac app and browser demo; five ways to control it; calibration that reports its own accuracy | Nobody with ALS has used it yet |
| Approvals, Stop and the help alert, covered by automated tests | Gaze accuracy measured only in simulation |
| Camera video stays on the computer; no tracking | Mac app not yet signed; real texts untested |
| 1st place, Cloud AI track, UCSC AI Hackathon | Infrared tracker support; Windows and iPad |

## What we're asking for

`[[TEAM: tailor per reader; keep one line each]]`

- **Clinicians:** 30 minutes to tell us what would stop you recommending it.
- **People with ALS and caregivers:** paid advisory roles to shape what we build.
- **Equipment programs:** a short loan of an infrared eye tracker so we can measure AGENCY honestly.
- **Funders:** support for a study with people with ALS and a free, signed release.

## Our principles

Free for users, always. Built with people with ALS, not just for them. Honest about what's tested. The person
decides; the AI asks first. Not a medical device.

## Team

Neil Gilani, Rayansh "Ray" Bakshi and Ryan Cho, students at UC Santa Cruz. We built AGENCY first for a team member's
grandmother, and are forming a nonprofit to give it away.
